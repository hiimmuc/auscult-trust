## Setup and metrics

- ICBHI official 60/40 split: 4,142 training and 2,756 test cycles; 4-class training. Seeds 0-4; every number is
  mean ± SD over the 5 seeds.
- Encoders AST, HTS-AT, BEATs, DASS, OPERA-CT; 8 fine-tuned (ft) and 6 frozen methods, each with its reference settings
  (`experiments/train/grid/<method>.yaml`). `ce_pafa_cfg` = CE with PAFA's settings (batch 32, no SpecAugment, 5 s cycles
  on AST/BEATs, BEATs 100 epochs): the control that separates PAFA's losses from its settings.
- Last epoch = primary. Best epoch on test = per seed the epoch with the best test Score: optimistic, for comparison with
  papers only.
- Columns, all in %: Sp = normal recall; Se = recall of crackle, wheeze, both; Score = (Sp + Se) / 2; HS = harmonic mean of
  Sp and Se. 2c = normal vs abnormal view of the same predictions (2c Sp = Sp). Worst-dev Score = lowest per-device Score.
  Cov = LAC split-conformal coverage at 90%, 20 device-stratified calibration/evaluation halves of the test patients;
  worst-dev Cov = lowest per-device coverage; set size = mean classes per set (of 4).

## Incidents

- QLung: the reference quality score is NaN for 71 AKGC417L cycles (leading digital silence); our port leaves silent
  frames out. No NaN in any unit.
- SG-SCL: the reference reversal schedule (weight 1 from step 0) collapsed BEATs in 3 of 4 seeds; all SG-SCL cells use
  the DANN ramp. Old results in `outputs/train/grid-v1-sgscl-ref/`.
- Out of memory on A at launch (15/10): fixed by budgeting summed reservations; none since.
- Power cut 17/10 10:43 (both machines, 9.6 h down). 13 units resumed from `last.pth` (RNG state not restored) were rerun
  without interruption and swapped in. Resumed vs rerun Score: mean +0.16, mean absolute 0.61, largest 2.5. Resumed
  results in `outputs|checkpoints/train/grid-v1-resumed/`; 5 reruns ran on the other machine's GPU.
- Train loss and accuracy per epoch are in `report.json` for 246 of 350 units (added 16/10); earlier units have them in
  their job logs.

## Limitations

- One split and 5 seeds: a 1-point difference is within seed noise for most cells.
- The last epoch is the reference schedule's, not a validated epoch; the best epoch is test-selected.
- Cells differ in batch size, cycle length and SpecAugment as well as in the method; only PAFA has a settings control.
- Worst-device numbers rest on the Littmann 3200 test set (461 cycles).
- GPU numerics are not bit-reproducible (`cudnn.benchmark`).
