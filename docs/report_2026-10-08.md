# Training sweep report, status on 2026-10-08 (15:45)

Scope: ICBHI 4-class respiratory sound classification, official 60/40 split. Part 1 (encoder comparison under the published protocols) is finished except for one DASS cell, Part 2 (proposed device-shift methods) has its cross-validation screening done and its final refit running. The refit tables are not in this report yet.

## 1. Setup

- **Data.** ICBHI 2017 (920 recordings, 6,898 cycles, 126 patients), official train/test split (4,142 / 2,756 cycles), 8 s cycles at 16 kHz. Classes: normal, crackle, wheeze, both. The 2-class score is a view of the same 4-class predictions (normal vs abnormal).
- **Metrics.** Sp = normal recall, Se = recall of abnormal cycles (correct class), Score = (Sp + Se) / 2, HS = harmonic mean of Sp and Se, per-class recall.
- **Training recipe** (Patch-Mix CL reference code): Adam, learning rate 5e-5, cosine schedule, batch 8 (DASS: 16, as in Lung-SRAD), 50 epochs, moving average 0.5, SpecAugment (160 frames, 48 bins), weight decay 1e-6. Patch-Mix CL: beta 1.0, tau 0.06 (DASS: 0.2), alpha 1.0.
- **Encoders.** AST, HTS-AT and DASS are trained in two modes (full fine-tuning, frozen encoder with trained head and projector); OPERA-CT, CLAP and HeAR only frozen. Each encoder gets its own native spectrogram (audited against the reference code; two mismatches found and fixed: HTS-AT now uses 10 s clips, DASS normalises each cycle). Patch-Mix is applied to the patch tokens of every encoder.
- **Protocol.** Seeds 0-4 (the papers use five seeds, no cross-validation). Numbers are mean ± SD over seeds at the epoch with the best test Score ("best on test", optimistic, as in the papers) and, in the last column, at epoch 50 (not selected on test).
- **Compute.** Two machines (RTX 5000 Ada and RTX 5090, 32 GB each); jobs run in parallel under a memory-gated scheduler and are resumable.

Findings:

- **Fine-tuning.** AST, HTS-AT and DASS fall within about 1 point of each other (60.5-61.6, seed SD 0.5-1.3). The data do not rank them. Patch-Mix CL helps AST by about 2 points (61.4 vs 59.5) and does not help HTS-AT or DASS (DASS is 1.1 points higher with Patch-Mix alone).
- **Frozen encoders.** AST 56.6-57.3 > DASS 55.2-56.1 > CLAP 53.8-54.9, HTS-AT 53.1-54.2, HeAR 53.3-53.7 > OPERA-CT 49.6-51.5. Fine-tuning gains 4-8 points over freezing for the same encoder.
- **Class balance.** HeAR and OPERA-CT reach their Score through high Sp and very low Se (12-25) and almost never predict wheeze or both. AST, HTS-AT and DASS recover more abnormal cycles. DASS has the best wheeze recall (49-54) and the best Se (47-49) among fine-tuned encoders.
- **Epoch selection matters.** Best-on-test epochs are often 1-10, and Score at epoch 50 is about 3 points lower than the best-on-test value for fine-tuned runs. Published best-on-test numbers therefore overstate what a fixed training length achieves.

## 4. Part 2: proposed device-shift methods, cross-validation screening (AST, Patch-Mix CL)

Three-fold patient-grouped cross-validation on the training patients (the test set is not used). Each spectrum-correction (SC) arm was run with both coefficient bounds (dynamic, static); the better bound per arm is kept by the registered rule.

| Arm | Cell kept | Bound | CV epoch | Mean CV Score | Fold Scores | Gap to baseline |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **SC (selected: AuscultTrust)** | sc + static | static | 14 | **60.51** | 52.2, 66.9, 62.3 | +1.54 |
| SC + random gain | sc_gain | dynamic | 2 | 60.01 | 54.5, 65.2, 60.4 | +1.04 |
| Baseline | baseline | - | 2 | 58.97 | 52.9, 64.0, 60.0 | 0 |
| Freq-MixStyle | freq_mixstyle | - | 3 | 58.71 | 52.4, 61.3, 62.5 | -0.26 |

Other cells: SC with the dynamic bound 58.44 (epoch 26); SC + random gain with the static bound 59.36 (epoch 1).

[Interpretation] The registered rule selects SC with the static bound. Three caveats: only three folds, with fold Scores spanning 52-67, so gaps of 1-1.5 points are inside the spread; the best CV epoch is 1-3 for several arms, which points to noise peaks; averaged over epochs 10-50 the paired gaps to the baseline are +1.3 (SC static), +1.7 (SC + random gain, static), -0.1 (SC dynamic), -0.2 (SC + random gain, dynamic) and +0.5 (Freq-MixStyle). So the static bound looks consistently useful and the dynamic bound and the random gain do not. The rule was not changed after seeing this.
